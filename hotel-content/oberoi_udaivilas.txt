Mewar by Vineet - Indian cuisine, Lunch 12:30 pm to 2:30 pm, Dinner 7:00 pm to 10:30 pm. Phone: +91 294 268 2301. Chef Vineet Bhatia (MBE) creates contemporary Indian cuisine inspired by the historic Mewar kingdom.

The Bar - Hours: 12:00 pm to 12:00 am. Phone: +91 294 268 2301. Traditional archways and domes with garden and lake views, offering premium beverages.

The Promenade - Hours: 6:00 pm to midnight. Exclusive to resident guests. Lakeside al fresco bar with breathtaking views of the old city by the banks of Lake Pichola, featuring grilled specialties.
